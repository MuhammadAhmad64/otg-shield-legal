# OTG Shield – Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** 15 November 2026 **App:** OTG Shield – USB Locker ## 1. Introduction OTG Shield – USB Locker ("the App") is designed to help users encrypt selected files and folders into password-protected vault containers. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, and what choices users have. ## 2. Information We Process Depending on the features you use, the App may process: - **Username:** Your chosen account username, used to identify your account and support account recovery. - **Authentication information:** Firebase Authentication may create an anonymous authentication identifier to support secure communication with Firebase services. - **Encrypted recovery information:** The App may upload an encrypted representation of your vault encryption key, together with the cryptographic metadata required for recovery. - **Selected files and folders:** Files and folders are accessed only when you select them for encryption or restoration. - **Technical information:** Firebase and Android services may process technical information necessary to provide, secure, and maintain their services. ## 3. File Encryption and Storage Files selected by you are encrypted on your device before the resulting vault container is saved to a location you choose. The App does not automatically encrypt every file on a connected USB drive or SD card. Original files are not automatically deleted after encryption. You remain responsible for deciding whether to retain, move, or delete the original files after verifying the encrypted copy. ## 4. Passwords and Encryption Your vault password is used locally to protect access to encrypted vault data. The App is designed not to upload your plaintext master password to Firebase. Cloud recovery uses encrypted key material rather than storing your master password as readable text. If you lose the password or required recovery credentials, encrypted data may become permanently inaccessible. ## 5. Cloud Services The App uses Firebase services for account authentication and cloud recovery. Firebase may process account identifiers and encrypted recovery records to provide these features. Encrypted recovery records are intended to protect the vault key from being stored in plaintext. Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy terms. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 6. Data Sharing We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase when necessary to operate authentication and cloud recovery features, or where disclosure is required by law. ## 7. Advertising and Analytics The V1.0 release is intended to contain no advertising SDK and no third-party advertising functionality. If advertising or analytics features are introduced in a future release, this policy and the applicable Play Console disclosures will be updated as necessary. ## 8. Data Retention and Deletion Account and cloud recovery information may remain stored until you request deletion or use the App's available account deletion feature. You can use the in-app account deletion option to request removal of the account and associated cloud recovery data supported by the App. Encrypted vault files previously saved to USB drives, SD cards, phones, computers, or other locations are not automatically deleted when an account is deleted. You must delete those files separately if you no longer need them. ## 9. Security The App uses encryption to protect selected files and encrypted recovery information. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. You should keep your passwords secure and maintain independent backups of important data. ## 10. Children's Privacy The App is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. ## 11. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available through the published Privacy Policy URL. ## 12.
