Privacy Policy

OTG Shield – USB Locker

Effective date: November 15, 2026

TACreations ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how OTG Shield – USB Locker handles information when you use our Android application.

Privacy at a glance

Your selected files are encrypted on your device. The app does not upload the contents of your files or encrypted vault containers to our cloud recovery service.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle the following information:

  • Account information: Your chosen username and account-related information required to identify your recovery record.
  • Cloud recovery information: A Firebase authentication identifier and encrypted recovery data, such as an encrypted key envelope and the cryptographic metadata required to use it.
  • Files selected by you: Files and folders that you choose to encrypt or restore using the app. These are accessed to perform the operation you request.
  • Technical information: Firebase or Android services may process limited technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

2. Files and USB / SD Card Access

OTG Shield uses Android's file selection and storage access features to let you select files and folders from supported removable storage, including USB drives and SD cards.

The app processes the files you select to create or restore encrypted vault containers. Your original files are not automatically deleted after encryption. You remain responsible for deciding how to handle the original copies.

Ordinary files that have not been encrypted remain accessible through other compatible file managers. The app does not claim to impose a universal operating-system-level USB lock.

3. Encryption and Password Security

Files selected for protection are encrypted on your device before the resulting vault container is saved to the storage location you choose.

Your Master Password is used as part of the local cryptographic protection process. We do not intend to store your Master Password in readable form on our servers. Cloud recovery data is stored in encrypted form and is designed to be unlocked using your credentials.

If you lose the credentials required to unlock a vault and have no usable backup, we may be unable to recover its contents. You should keep your credentials safe.

4. Firebase and Cloud Recovery

The app uses Google Firebase services to support account identification and cloud recovery functionality.

When you use cloud recovery, the app may send account-related identifiers and encrypted recovery information to Firebase. This information allows the app to locate and retrieve the encrypted recovery record when you sign in on another device.

Your original documents, photos, videos, and other file contents are not uploaded to Firebase by the cloud recovery feature. The encrypted vault files remain under your control in the storage location where you save them.

Firebase is provided by Google. Its processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Information

Information handled by the app is used to:

  • Provide account identification and recovery features.
  • Encrypt and restore files selected by you.
  • Retrieve encrypted recovery information when requested.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Diagnose technical problems with the services.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase where necessary to provide the app's cloud recovery functionality.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of our services.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Encrypted vault files are stored in the location you choose, such as a USB drive, SD card, or another supported storage location. Cloud recovery records may remain in Firebase until they are deleted through a supported app feature or following a verified deletion request.

Deleting the app does not necessarily delete vault files saved on removable storage or cloud recovery records. If you want your cloud recovery information deleted, contact us using the email address below.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You control which files and folders you select for encryption or restoration. You may stop using cloud recovery and may request deletion of account-related cloud data by contacting us.

Please include your app username and sufficient information for us to identify the relevant recovery record. Do not send your Master Password or any secret recovery credentials by email.

Deleting cloud recovery information does not automatically delete encrypted vault files stored on your USB drive, SD card, phone, or computer.

9. Children's Privacy

OTG Shield is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

10. Security

We use encryption and reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should keep their devices, passwords, and encrypted backups protected.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
