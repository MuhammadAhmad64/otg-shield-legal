Privacy Policy

OTG Shield – USB Locker

Effective date: November 18, 2026

TACreations ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how OTG Shield – USB Locker handles information when you use our Android application, including its file encryption, cloud recovery, and advertising features.

Privacy at a glance

Your selected files are encrypted on your device. The app does not upload your original file contents or encrypted vault containers to its cloud recovery service. The app uses Google Firebase for account and recovery functionality and Meta Audience Network to display advertisements.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the app and its third-party service providers may handle the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Your chosen username and account-related information required to identify your recovery record.
  • Cloud recovery information: A Firebase authentication identifier and encrypted recovery data, such as an encrypted key envelope and the cryptographic metadata required to use it.
  • Files selected by you: Files and folders that you choose to encrypt or restore using the app. These are accessed to perform the operation you request.
  • Advertising and device information: When advertisements are requested or displayed, Meta Audience Network may process information such as device identifiers or advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), IP address, device and operating system information, app-related information, ad delivery data, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic data.
  • Technical information: Firebase, Android, and advertising services may process limited technical, network, usage, and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, measure, and troubleshoot their services.

2. Files and USB / SD Card Access

OTG Shield uses Android's file selection and storage access features to let you select files and folders from supported removable storage, including USB drives and SD cards.

The app processes the files you select to create or restore encrypted vault containers. Your original files are not automatically deleted after encryption. You remain responsible for deciding how to handle the original copies.

Ordinary files that have not been encrypted remain accessible through other compatible file managers. The app does not claim to impose a universal operating-system-level USB lock.

3. Encryption and Password Security

Files selected for protection are encrypted on your device before the resulting vault container is saved to the storage location you choose.

Your Master Password is used as part of the local cryptographic protection process. We do not intend to store your Master Password in readable form on our servers. Cloud recovery data is stored in encrypted form and is designed to be unlocked using your credentials.

The app does not intentionally send your Master Password, original file contents, or plaintext vault contents to Meta Audience Network for advertising purposes.

If you lose the credentials required to unlock a vault and have no usable backup, we may be unable to recover its contents. You should keep your credentials safe.

4. Firebase and Cloud Recovery

The app uses Google Firebase services to support account identification and cloud recovery functionality.

When you use cloud recovery, the app may send account-related identifiers and encrypted recovery information to Firebase. This information allows the app to locate and retrieve the encrypted recovery record when you sign in on another device.

Your original documents, photos, videos, and other file contents are not uploaded to Firebase by the cloud recovery feature. The encrypted vault files remain under your control in the storage location where you save them.

Firebase is provided by Google. Its processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising and Meta Audience Network

OTG Shield uses Meta Audience Network, a third-party advertising service, to display advertisements within the application.

Depending on the app version, available inventory, and supported ad formats, advertisements may include banner, native banner, medium rectangle, and interstitial ads.

When an advertisement is requested, displayed, or interacted with, Meta and its advertising technology providers may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and device-related identifiers.
  • IP address and general network information.
  • Device model, operating system, and technical information.
  • Advertisement requests, impressions, and interactions.
  • Ad performance, fraud prevention, and diagnostic information.

This information may be used for advertisement delivery, frequency management, measurement, reporting, fraud prevention, and other advertising-related purposes.

Depending on your location, applicable privacy requirements, available settings, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised.

Meta Audience Network operates independently as a third-party service. Its processing of information is governed by Meta's applicable privacy terms and policies.

For more information about Meta's data practices, please review: Meta Privacy Policy .

You can review available advertising and privacy choices through your device settings and the privacy controls provided by Meta, where applicable.

6. How We Use Information

Information handled by the app and its service providers may be used to:

  • Provide account identification and recovery features.
  • Encrypt and restore files selected by you.
  • Retrieve encrypted recovery information when requested.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Prevent advertising fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Diagnose technical problems with the services.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers where necessary to provide the app's features.

  • Google Firebase: Processes account-related and encrypted recovery information to support cloud recovery functionality.
  • Meta Audience Network: Processes advertising-related and technical information to deliver, measure, and support advertisements.

These third-party providers process information under their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of our services.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Encrypted vault files are stored in the location you choose, such as a USB drive, SD card, or another supported storage location. Cloud recovery records may remain in Firebase until they are deleted through a supported app feature or following a verified deletion request.

Advertising-related information may be retained by Meta and its service providers according to their own retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

Deleting the app does not necessarily delete vault files saved on removable storage or cloud recovery records. It also does not automatically delete information already processed by third-party advertising providers.

If you want your cloud recovery information deleted, contact us using the email address below.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You control which files and folders you select for encryption or restoration. You may stop using cloud recovery and may request deletion of account-related cloud data by contacting us.

Please include your app username and sufficient information for us to identify the relevant recovery record. Do not send your Master Password or any secret recovery credentials by email.

Deleting cloud recovery information does not automatically delete encrypted vault files stored on your USB drive, SD card, phone, or computer.

Requests relating to advertising data collected directly by Meta may need to be submitted through Meta's own privacy tools and controls.

10. Children's Privacy

OTG Shield is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

11. Security

We use encryption and reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should keep their devices, passwords, and encrypted backups protected.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect information under our control, we cannot guarantee the security practices or systems of independent third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
