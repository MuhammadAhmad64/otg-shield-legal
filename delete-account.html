Account Deletion

OTG Shield – USB Locker
Want to delete your OTG Shield account?

Use the button below to email TACreations and request deletion of your account and associated cloud data. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap Send account deletion request.
  2. Enter your OTG Shield username in the email message.
  3. Provide an email address where we can contact you about the request.
  4. Send the email. TACreations may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing deletion.
✉   Send account deletion request

If your device does not open an email app, send your request manually to tacreations380@gmail.com with the subject OTG Shield – Account Deletion Request.

What will be deleted
  • Your OTG Shield account/recovery record and associated cloud data that TACreations can identify and control, after ownership verification.
  • Any account-related information held by TACreations for this app, unless retention is required for a legitimate legal or security reason. If anything must be retained, the reason and applicable retention period will be explained.

What this request does not delete

  • Encrypted vault files: Files such as .vault or .otgvault saved on a USB drive, SD card, computer, or another storage location are not remotely erased by deleting your app account. You must manage or securely delete those copies yourself.
  • Files on your device: Data stored locally on a device you control may require you to remove the app or clear its app data after the account deletion is completed.
Security warning

Never include your Master Password, vault password, PIN, recovery key, or any other secret in an email or deletion request. TACreations will never ask you to send these credentials.

Processing and confirmation

We will review the request, may ask for reasonable account-ownership verification, and will confirm the outcome by email. Requests are handled as promptly as reasonably possible. If any data must be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason, we will explain what is retained and why.
